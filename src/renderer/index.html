    slopamp.com·GitHub

    A retro-styled player for your own media files plus YouTube videos through YouTube’s official embedded player. The DOWNLOAD button saves a copy with yt-dlp — only download videos you have the right to save.

    SlopAMP is free software (MIT). It bundles Electron/Chromium and FFmpeg (LGPL v3). It includes the Butterchurn visualizer (MIT) and its preset collection. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Winamp, VideoLAN, or YouTube/Google.
