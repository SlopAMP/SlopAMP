## What it does

**▶ Plays your stuff.** Local music and videos, **PLUS YouTube links, right in the app** (YouTube, YouTube Music and YouTube Live). Paste a link and it plays. No browser needed.

**⬇ Keeps your stuff.** Download as video or MP3 to keep offline. No browser tab needed.

**⚙ Under the hood.** [Electron](https://www.electronjs.org/), [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/), [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) and [Butterchurn](https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn).

## Features

- **Playlist tabs**: juggle multiple playlists at once.
- **Save / Load / Quick Save**: playlists as `.m3u8`.
- **Manage Library**: search and manage downloads and saved playlists.
- **Equalizer**: push the sliders. Feel things.
- **Resume**: picks up where you left off.
- **Playback speed**: 1x to 3x.
- **Subtitles**: for the videos.
- **Media keys**: your keyboard's play button finally matters.
- **Fullscreen + compact**: go big, or go narrow classic-sized.
- **Visualizer**: powered by Butterchurn, the free open-source MilkDrop engine, with 1,700+ presets plus favorites.

## Install

1. Download **SlopAMP-Setup-1.1.0.exe** from [Releases](https://github.com/SlopAMP/SlopAMP/releases/latest).
2. Run it.

> **Heads up:** SlopAMP isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen). Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
