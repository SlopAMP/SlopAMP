# Code of Conduct

SlopAMP is a small hobby project, and we want working on it to be friendly for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to programming.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.
- If you made a mistake, own it and move on. We all do it.

## What's not OK

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as inappropriate in a shared space.

## Where this applies

Everywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere you represent SlopAMP.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, use GitHub's **Report content** option (in the `…` menu of a comment, issue or pull request). The maintainers see these reports and handle them privately. Please don't use this for security bugs: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for those.

## What happens next

The maintainers decide what to do. Depending on how serious it is, that can be a reminder, removing comments, locking a thread, or blocking someone from the project. We'll keep the reporter's identity private.

This code of conduct is based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
